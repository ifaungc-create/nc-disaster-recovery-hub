SNAP Eligibility Has Changed: What Neighbors Need to Know
The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), also known as Food and Nutrition Services (FNS) in North Carolina
, helps individuals and families afford groceries when budgets are tight. SNAP is an essential part of the broader network of food assistance available to neighbors facing food insecurity.
