NOT A GOVERNMENT APP. NC Disaster Recovery Hub is a free project of C4I Veteran Services Corp., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It is not a government agency and does not represent or act for FEMA, the State of North Carolina, any county or any agency listed in the app. It does not take applications for government benefits. In an emergency, call 911.

NC Disaster Recovery Hub helps people in North Carolina find help after a hurricane, flood, fire or other disaster, and keep track of it. To apply for help or confirm details, use the official websites below or call the agency.

What the app does
- County emergency management, social services, health departments and fire departments for all 100 counties, with phone numbers and official links
- Shelter and housing help, nearby hotels and motels, free legal aid, insurance help, replacing lost documents, food and home-care steps after a fire or flood
- Private notes for case, claim and application numbers, encrypted and visible only to you; download or delete your data at any time
- Optional alerts for the counties you choose (not an emergency alert system)
- English and Spanish; built to WCAG 2.1 AA; no ads, no tracking, no analytics
