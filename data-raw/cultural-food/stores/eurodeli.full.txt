About Us
Euro Deli Mart - We Pride Ourselves on a Wide Assortment of Quality Products and Outstanding Service!
Euro Deli Mart features an extensive selection of imported Polish, Central and Eastern European food favorites including
Pierogi, Smoked Kielbasa, Fresh Polish Sausages, Ham and other Cold Cuts, Cheeses, Stuffed Cabbage, Breads, Cakes and more
!
We carry a wide assortment of canned and packaged goods including sauerkraut, pickles, mushrooms, mayonnaise, mustard, ketchups, soups, spices and salad dressings. We also carry a large assortment of canned meats, pate, sardines, mackerel, and marinated herring.
We feature a variety of baking products including flours, flavorings and mixes, as well as jams and jellies. Our dairy case includes a selection of European butters, cheeses and yogurts. And our freezer includes many favorites like pierogi, prepared meals and breads. Our beverages include carbonated and still waters, juices, syrups, coffees and teas.
And for a quick treat, we offer a wide assortment of candies, chocolates, frozen treats, chips, pretzels, cookies, baked goods and more.
