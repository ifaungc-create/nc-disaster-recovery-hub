NOT A GOVERNMENT APP. NC Disaster Recovery Hub is a free project of C4I Veteran Services Corp., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It is not a government agency. It does not represent, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorized by, FEMA, the State of North Carolina, any county or any other government entity. It does not take applications for government benefits. In an emergency, call 911.

NC Disaster Recovery Hub helps people in North Carolina find help after a hurricane, flood, fire or other disaster. All government information in this app comes from the official sources listed here.

WHAT THE APP DOES
- Phone numbers and links for county emergency management, social services, health and fire departments
- Shelter, housing, legal aid, insurance help, replacing documents, and steps after a fire or flood
- Private notes for your case and claim numbers; delete your data at any time
- Optional county alerts (not an emergency alert system)
- English and Spanish. No ads and no tracking.

Information can change; confirm with the agency. Questions or a wrong number? Email info@c4iveteranservices.org
