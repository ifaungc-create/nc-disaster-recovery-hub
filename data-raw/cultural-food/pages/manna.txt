										What Is a Food Bank?									
A food bank is different from a food pantry – rather than handing out free food directly to those in need, MANNA delivers bulk food to 300+ partner food pantries, meal sites, and schools. As a distribution hub for the WNC charitable food system, over 95% of the food we serve goes through our partners.

										One Voice Can Spark a Movement									
MANNA FoodBank is a nonpartisan, nonprofit organization dedicated to educating the public about issues that could impact our neighbors now and in the future.
MANNA supports legislation that views food as a basic human right and supports our neighbors experiencing food insecurity. Stand with us, and your voice can influence policies that grant more access to food, drive systemic change, and make us all stronger.
