We believe everyone deserves to eat.
Ac
ross our 18-county service area,
requests for food assistance have doubled
from 2022 levels and remain elevated. Families are still rebuilding from Hurricane Helene. Many have not yet recovered from pandemic-era losses. Now, with disruptions in critical nutrition programs, the pressure is on our network and our community to stand together. Because everyone deserves to eat.
