Programs, advocacy, and community engagement that
make a difference.
For more than 30 years, The Hispanic Liaison / El Vínculo Hispano has delivered programs and advocacy rooted in the real needs of the Hispanic and Latinx community.
Advocacy & Civic Engagement
Empowering our community to participate fully in civic life and advocate for their rights.
Community
Defense
Standing alongside community members facing legal challenges and protecting immigrant rights.
Orgullo
Latinx Pride
Building pride, leadership and mental wellness for Latinx youth.
Community
Empowerment
Connecting individuals and families to essential services and resources for self-sufficiency.
Hispanic Arts
& Culture
Celebrating the rich cultural heritage of our Liatinx community.
Collaborative
Garden
Cultivating community through green space and sustainable growing practices.
Over 30 years strengthening families, youth, and community leaders.

🎓 ROUND 3 of celebrating our OLP Class of 2026!
Our next senior spotlight features Ashley Herrera, a graduate of Jordan-Matthews High School! 🎉 Ashley is attending the University of North Carolina at Greensboro this fall, where she will pursue a degree in biology! 🧬🔬
Next up is Ana Nicole Banegas, a graduate of Seaforth High School! 🎓 She will continue her studies at Central Carolina Community College (CCCC), with plans to transfer to a four-year university to pursue a career in architecture! 🏛️📐
Congratulations, Ashley and Ana Nicole! 💚 Our OLP team thanks you both for your dedication to the program and will continue cheering you on as you begin this exciting new chapter! 🌟

🎓 ¡Seguimos celebrando a nuestra generación OLP 2026!
¡Nuestra siguiente destacada es Ashley Herrera, graduada de Jordan-Matthews High School! 🎉 Este otoño, Ashley comenzará sus estudios de biología en la Universidad de Carolina del Norte en Greensboro (UNCG). 🧬🔬
¡También celebramos a Ana Nicole Banegas, graduada de Seaforth High School! 🎓 Ana Nicole continuará sus estudios en Central Carolina Community College (CCCC) y espera transferirse a una universidad de cuatro años para estudiar arquitectura. 🏛️📐
¡Felicidades, Ashley y Ana Nicole! 💚 Todo el equipo de OLP les agradece su compromiso con el programa y seguirá apoyándolas y celebrando sus logros en esta nueva etapa de sus vidas. 🌟
