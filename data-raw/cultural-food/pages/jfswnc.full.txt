Emergency Services
Keren Ami Emergency Funds
JFS applies for and administers emergency funds from the WNC Jewish Federation’s Keren Ami Fund, which provides assistance to Jewish individuals and families with temporary needs for rent, utilities, and other financial emergencies and crisis situations.
Food Pantry
JFS maintains a food pantry with a variety of nutritious items for any individual or family needing temporary assistance
M-F 9am-4pm
. JFS is a partner agency with MANNA FoodBank. We also have Ingles gift cards for groceries and gas, availability based on need.
Call 828-253-2900 to set up an appointment with Carrie McGuire to discuss Keren Ami.
