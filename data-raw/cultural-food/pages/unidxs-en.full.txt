About Our Food Pantry
Our food pantry serves families in Western North Carolina by providing nutritious food and essential supplies. We believe that no one should go hungry, and we work to ensure that everyone in our community has access to the resources they need.
What We Offer
Open Pantry
Walk-in service where families can select the items they need. No appointment necessary.
Food Boxes
Pre-packed boxes with essential food items for families. Perfect for those with limited time.
Grab and Go
Quick pickup service for families on the go. Get what you need quickly and conveniently.
How You Can Help
Volunteer
Join our team of volunteers and help us serve the community. Contact Cristela to learn more.
Donate Items
We accept non-perishable foods, hygiene products, diapers, and other essential items.
Spread the Word
Help us reach more families by sharing information about our food pantry services.
