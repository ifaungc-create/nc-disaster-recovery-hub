Mobile Pop-Up Markets
Our Pop-Up Markets bring fresh produce and pantry staples directly to communities that do not have as many traditional pantry and meal site options. There are no residency, income, or ID requirements to participate, and neighbors are free to shop for whatever items they may need.
Find dates and locations near you
Support in Your Community
In addition to traditional pantries and meal sites, the Food Bank network offers a host of resources and to help families keep food on the table and get ahead.
SNAP Grocery Support
SNAP (FNS) is a lifeline for more than 1.4 million kids, seniors, people with disabilities, and working adults across North Carolina. Our team is here to help you access financial support to pay for groceries.
Get started with SNAP
Disaster Relief
From emergency food and water packages to ready-to-heat meals, we're ready to respond in the aftermath of major storms. Learn more about the resources available during hurricane season and beyond.
View storm resources
Senior Programs
The Commodity Supplemental Food Program provides monthly boxes of pantry staples for low-income individuals age 60+. We add fresh produce, dairy, and protein to help meet the dietary needs of seniors in our community.
Senior Programs
School & Summer Programs
Through school-based pantries, snacks at afterschool programs, take-home options for weekends, and breakfast and lunches at summer meal sites, our Child Nutrition team partners with local schools and community centers to provide support to families year-round.
Programs for kids and teens
Job Training Partnerships
Our workforce development partnerships provide hands-on education and training for individuals who are unemployed, underemployed, or justice involved. Together, we're dedicated to connecting talented people with in-demand career opportunities.
Explore training opportunities
Recipes & Nutrition Resources
Check out our Nutrition Team's compilation of cooking videos, kitchen how-to’s, nutrition guidelines, and printable health and nutrition information. There's no shortage of resources to provide healthy, affordable meals that everyone can enjoy!
