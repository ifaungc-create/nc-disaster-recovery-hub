The Community Mosque of Winston Salem invites every food insecure household to come out for distribution of fresh groceries.
Inflation has taken over our nation causing prices to rise in almost every food industry. This has increased the pressure on families already battling financial hardships. The goal of Al-Maun Food Bank is to aid in a way that will lessen the burden and replace it with HOPE!
The Al-Maun Food Bank is one of the only food banks that distributes an average of 70-150 food boxes every 3rd Saturday of each month.
If you know of anyone faced with this challenge, please send them to The Community Mosque of Winston Salem located at:
1419 Waughtown Street every 3rd Saturday
to receive groceries from 11:00am -12:30pm or while supplies last.
If you would like to join us in our effort, please donate by clicking on one of the links above.

The Community Mosque of WS provides weekly Islamic Studies Classes and Salaatul Jummah khutbahs in the Forsyth County Detention Center, the county jail that houses detainees awaiting trial and others serving sentences of two years or less. Qurans and other Islamic materials are provided to the Muslim detainees and other men interested in Islam. Also, numerous brothers in the Community Mosque are qualified by the NC Department of Corrections as certified sponsors, allowing them to take inmates housed in the city’s state minimum security facility, Forsyth Advancement Center, out for 4-hour sponsor passes.

The Community Mosque Academy is a weekend school for Muslim children aged four to 18. Children are instructed in Qur’an, Hadith, Seerah, Arabic, and Adab (Good manners). As a supplement to classes at the Community Mosque Academy, a bi-weekly discussion group meets to allow students to freely discuss personal issues and concerns with the Youth Director of the mosque in an environment of confidentiality. Group members also take field trips, watch Islamic-themed movies, and play games.
