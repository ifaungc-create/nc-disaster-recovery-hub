NOT A GOVERNMENT APP. NC Disaster Recovery Hub is a free project of C4I Veteran Services Corp., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It is not a government agency and does not represent or act for FEMA, the State of North Carolina, any county or any agency listed in the app. It does not take applications for government benefits. In an emergency, call 911.

NC Disaster Recovery Hub helps people in North Carolina find help after a hurricane, flood, fire or other disaster — and keep track of it.

Find help
• Your county's emergency management office, social services and nearby fire departments, with phone numbers and links
• Shelter and housing help, nearby hotels and motels with directions
• Free legal aid, insurance help and how to file a claim, replacing lost documents, food and cultural food pantries, home-care steps after a disaster
• Free translation apps, the translation built into your phone, and how to get a free interpreter

Keep track
• Save case, claim and application numbers (FEMA, insurance and more), with who to call and when
• Private notes and referrals, stored encrypted and never shared
• Download your data or delete your account at any time

Alerts (optional)
• Turn on notifications for statewide alerts or for the counties you choose; turn them off any time
• This is not an emergency alert system: keep Wireless Emergency Alerts on and call 911 in an emergency

Built for everyone
• English and Spanish
• Built to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines
• Resource lists keep working offline after your first visit
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
