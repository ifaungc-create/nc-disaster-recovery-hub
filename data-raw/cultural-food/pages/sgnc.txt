Gurpurab celebrations include either Sahaj Paath or Akhand Paath recited by members of Sangat. Keertan programs are arranged on Friday and Saturday evenings and Sunday mornings. Generally a visiting Ragi Jathaa is invited. Emails are sent on a timely basis to announce GurPurab programs.
