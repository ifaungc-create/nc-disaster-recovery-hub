Discover the Heart of Kim's Asian Market
Bringing Authentic Asian Flavors to Your Table
Welcome to Kim's Asian Market, a family-owned business rooted in South Korea, dedicated to delivering authentic Asian groceries to your table. Our journey began with a love for the rich culinary traditions of our homeland, inspiring us to share these flavors in Asheville through our store.
We specialize in homemade kimchi, crafted with care from a family recipe spanning generations. Our commitment to authenticity and quality makes our kimchi a favorite among customers, embodying the taste of South Korea.
At Kim's Asian Market, we strive to provide a diverse selection of the finest Asian groceries and create a welcoming environment for all. Visit us to explore our range of products and experience our dedication to sharing the joy of Asian cuisine.
Thank you for being part of our journey. We look forward to serving you and enhancing your culinary experiences with the authentic flavors of Asia.

Frequently Asked Questions
Find answers to common questions about our Korean grocery store and services.
What are your store hours?
We are open from 9:30am to 7:30pm Monday to Saturday and 10:30am to 6:00pm Sunday.
What forms of payment do you accept?
We accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, and EBT.
Do you have a return policy?
Yes, we accept returns within 7 days with a receipt for all dry items. The product must be unopened and in its original condition.
What types of products do you carry?
We offer a wide variety of Asian groceries, including fresh produce, spices, sauces, noodles, snacks, beverages, and more.
Do you carry products from specific countries?
Yes, we stock products from several Asian countries including Korea, Japan, China, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, and India.
Do you carry organic products?
Yes, we offer a range of organic products.
Can I place a special order for an item not in stock?
Yes, please contact us, and we will do our best to source the item for you.
